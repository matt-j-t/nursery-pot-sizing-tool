Nursery pot sizing tool

Measure the inside of your decorative pot. I'll shrink those numbers into a liner that fits, with room to lift it back out.

Know the bottom diameter? Add it and we'll account for how much the pot tapers. Don't know it, or can't easily reach in to measure? Skip it — we'll model the liner without that taper.

Lift notches (rim finger grips)
Air slots (root air-pruning)

Lift notches are a fixed 20mm-wide, 6mm-deep finger recess at the rim — sized to a finger, not the pot. Air slots are fixed-width (2–4mm) vertical slots in the bottom half of the wall; the count scales with pot size.
