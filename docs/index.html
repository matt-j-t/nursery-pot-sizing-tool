Nursery Pot Sizing Tool

Round, tapered 3D-printed liner pots that fit inside a decorative pot. Tuned for a Bambu Lab P2S, 0.4mm nozzle, PETG.

Measure the INSIDE of your decorative pot — not its outside — and I'll shrink those numbers down to a liner pot that fits inside it, with room to lift back out.

Just the top opening and depth — good enough when the pot doesn't taper much, or you can't easily reach in to measure the bottom. For pots that narrow a lot near the base, use "Full" instead so the taper is calculated correctly.

Optional: drop a decorative pot STL here (or click to choose a file) to auto-fill the fields below from its inner cavity.

Measure (or detect) the INSIDE of the decorative pot — not its outside — since that's the cavity the nursery pot needs to fit into.

Advanced options (design rule overrides)

Lift notches are a fixed 20mm-wide, 6mm-deep finger recess at the rim — sized to a finger, not the pot, so they don't scale with pot size.
